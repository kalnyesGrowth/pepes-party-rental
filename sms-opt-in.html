SMS Opt-In Disclosure

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Program Description

Pepe's Party Rental LLC uses SMS text messaging to communicate with customers about their party rental bookings. By providing your phone number and opting in to receive text messages, you consent to receive the following types of messages from Pepe's Party Rental:

  • Booking confirmations
  • Delivery and pickup reminders
  • Appointment reminders (48-hour and 2-hour before event)
  • Order status updates
  • Weather-related notifications affecting your rental
  • Follow-up messages and review requests after your event
  • Promotional offers and seasonal specials (with separate consent)

How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Pepe's Party Rental by:

  • Providing your phone number on our booking form and checking the SMS consent box
  • Texting a keyword (e.g., PARTY) to our business number
  • Verbally consenting during a phone call with our team

By opting in, you agree to the following:

I consent to receive automated text messages from Pepe's Party Rental LLC at the phone number I provided. I understand that message frequency varies and that message and data rates may apply. I understand that my consent is not a condition of any purchase. I can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your booking activity. Typically you will receive:

  • 1-3 messages per booking (confirmation, reminder, follow-up)
  • Promotional messages: no more than 4 per month

Costs

Pepe's Party Rental does not charge for SMS messages. However, message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply. Check with your carrier for details about your text messaging plan.

How to Opt Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message you receive from us
  • Contacting us at pepespartyr@gmail.com
  • Calling us at (571) 244-5255

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message. You will no longer receive any SMS messages from us unless you opt in again. Opting out of SMS does not cancel any existing bookings.

Privacy

Your phone number and personal information are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is only shared with our SMS service provider for the purpose of delivering messages.

Supported Carriers

SMS messaging is supported on all major US carriers including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, and most regional carriers. Carrier fees and limitations may vary.
