How Do I Book the Party Barrel for My Event?

Booking the Party Barrel takes under 5 minutes. Pepe's Party Rental requires no deposit and offers free rescheduling for weather. Here is how to plan your event:

  1. Book 2-3 weeks ahead. Weekends from May through September fill up fastest. Holiday weekends (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day) require 3-4 weeks notice.
  2. Confirm your space. Authentic barrel style. Make sure you have flat, clear ground with clearance on all sides. Our team confirms space requirements when you book.
